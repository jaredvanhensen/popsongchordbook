Yes! Both the guitar lessons and the keyboard/piano lessons, as well as the hundreds of scrolling chord song timelines in PopSongChordBook, are completely free to use with no hidden fees.

Do I need to know how to read music?

No, absolutely not. Our method is built entirely visually around chord shapes, guitar chord diagrams, piano diagrams, and our real-time scrolling chord timeline. This is the fastest way to play along with pop music. Fast results! Play the music you love! That makes practicing so much more fun :-) Playing along with the original track is amazing for your timing and your musical ear, and it pushes you out of your comfort zone tempo faster. This is where you grow the quickest!

Does this work on my phone or tablet?

Absolutely! PopSongChordBook is a responsive web application (PWA) that works perfectly in any browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, and Mac. A widescreen display such as a tablet or laptop is recommended for the richest experience and full functionality. Due to the limited screen space on mobile phones, song editing is more restricted there.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Definitely. You can walk through lessons 1 to 9 at your own pace, answer questions in the interactive quizzes, and replay songs as many times as you like. You check off a lesson once you master it and are ready for the next one. You can even play along with your favorite songs at your own speed by simply slowing down the tempo.
