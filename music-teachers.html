Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Music Teachers Ask Us

💰 Is PopSongChordBook free to use?

Yes! PopSongChordBook provides free access to interactive scrolling chords, beginner lesson methods, and song libraries for both teachers and students.

📱 What devices does it work on?

It runs seamlessly on any device with a modern web browser (iPad, iPhone, Mac, Windows PC, Chromebooks) and on mobile with the official Android App from Google Play.

👥 How do students connect with me?

In your Teacher Dashboard, you have a unique Teacher ID and direct invite link. Share this link with your students, and once they accept, their dashboard automatically connects to yours.

🎸 Can I use it for Piano, Keyboard, Guitar, and Ukulele?

Yes! PopSongChordBook includes interactive chord overlays and visual voicing diagrams for piano/keyboard, guitar, and ukulele. You can switch instruments with a single tap.

🎼 Can I add my own custom songs?

Absolutely. You can import MIDI files, load songs via our Super Extractor, or create custom chord progressions with specific tempo, capo, and section markers for your students.

🔄 How does device sync work during online lessons?

Our real-time Band Practice Mode uses lightweight cloud sync. When you start the timeline or pause on your teacher device, the connected student's screen moves simultaneously, making Zoom or Google Meet lessons feel like you're in the same room.
